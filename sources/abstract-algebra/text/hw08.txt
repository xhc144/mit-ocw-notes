Some parenthetical remarks 
For some parts of questions 7–10, you may ﬁnd it useful to use the 
fact that R ⊕ S is both a direct product and a direct sum. We have 
already seen the deﬁnition of the direct product. The direct sum is the 
same as the direct product except that the arrows are reversed. That 
is, the direct sum R ⊕ S of R and S satisﬁes the following universal 
property:
