6. Conjugation in Sn 
One thing that is very easy to understand in terms of Sn is conjuga­
tion. 
Deﬁnition 6.1. Let g and h be two elements of a group G. 
The element ghg−1 is called the conjugate of h by g. 
One reason why conjugation is so important, is because it measures 
how far the group G is from being abelian. 
Indeed if G were abelian, then 
gh = hg. 
Multiplying by g−1 on the right, we would have 
h = ghg−1 . 
Thus G is abelian iﬀ the conjugate of every element by any other 
element is the same element. 
Another reason why conjugation is so important, is that really con­
jugation is the same as translation.

Deﬁnition 6.4. The equivalence classes of the equivalence relation 
above are called conjugacy classes. 
Given an aribtrary group G, it can be quite hard to determine the 
conjugacy classes of G. Here is the most that can be said in general. 
Lemma 6.5. Let G be a group. Then the conjugacy classes all have 
exactly one element iﬀ G is abelian. 
Proof. Easy exercise.
