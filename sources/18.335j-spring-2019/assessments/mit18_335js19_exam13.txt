Problem 1: GMRES (20 points)
From class, the GMRES algorithm iteratively builds up an orthonormal basis Qn for the Krylov space Kn =
spanhb, Ab, . . . , An−1 bi and then uses this basis to solve minx∈Kn kAx − bk2 .

  (a) We normally assume that each iteration n gives us a linearly independent vector, i.e. that An b is not
      in Kn . What happens if this is false, i.e. An b ∈ Kn (“breakdown”)? Show that in the (unlikely) event
      that this occurs, it is a good thing, not a bad thing, for solving Ax = b.

  (b) Given an m × m A (which you can assume to be diagonalizable), how would you (theoretically) con-
      struct a b such that breakdown occurs after n < m steps (in exact arithmetic)?
