Harmonic oscillators are ubiquitous in physics. HO behavior appears in any 
quadratic potential, and most physicists are happy to believe that if you look 
close enough, all potentials are quadratic. 
My aim here and in the next lecture is to explore forced and damped HOs; 
we will see non-linearities and parametric resonance only numerically, but you 
can read LL 23-24 for more info.
