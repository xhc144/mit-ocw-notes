Let’s move on to the next topic of this class. We’ve been talking about Young diagrams and partitions a lot so far - in 
this second half, we’ll start to discuss graphs, networks, and trees! (Graphs are objects with vertices and edges.) 
Deﬁnition 1 
A labeled tree is a connected graph on n nodes with no cycles, where the nodes are labeled 1, 2, · · · , n. Unlabeled 
trees are the analogous object where the nodes are not labeled. 
Example 2
