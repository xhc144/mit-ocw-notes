            HOMEWORK 5  (18.315, FALL 2005)


1) In the Eventown, there are 2n people and m clubs A1, . . . , Am such that | Ai | and | Ai ∩ Aj |
are even, for all 1 ≤ i, j ≤ m. Prove that m ≤ 2n .

2) Compute the probability that a random permutation σ ∈ Sn is an involution: σ2 = 1.
Compute the probability that two random permutations σ, ω ∈ Sn commute: σω = ωσ. Which
event is more likely?

3) Try to classify all ﬁnite connected planar vertex­transitive graphs.   (If you can’t ﬁnd all of
them – explain clearly what subclass of them you can classify.)

4) Prove or disprove the following result: Every plane triangulation without separating triangles
contains a Hamiltonian cycle. Here a triangle is called separating  if it is a triangle in a graph
but not a face.

Important:  If you can’t ﬁgure this out on your own, try to read Whitney’s article (see the web
page).
