Compute the GCD of the following two numbers:



 
  \(x=17^{88}\cdot 31^5\cdot 37^2\cdot 59^{1000} \\
    y=19^{9^{22}}\cdot 37^{12}\cdot 53^{3678}\cdot 59^{29}\)
  
 
 Use \(^\) for exponentiation and \(*\) for multiplication in your answer.

 
 Exercise 1 
   &nbsp;  Numerical Response     &nbsp;  

 

 


 
 To get the GCD of two numbers: iterate over all primes that appear in both factorizations;
       raise each of them to the  smallest  of its two exponents; then multiply the resulting powers.
    

 By the way, if in this procedure you replace the  smallest  with the  greatest , the return value
      will be the LCM (least common multiple) of the two numbers.
