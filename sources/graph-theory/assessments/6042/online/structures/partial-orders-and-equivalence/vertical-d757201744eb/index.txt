 Two different people can be the same age, so the relation is not antisymmetric, ruling out  partial order  and  total order . It is not symmetric, since a 4-year-old is related to a 2-year-old, but not conversely, ruling out  equivalence relation .
      Note that as a relation on their  ages , this would be the same as the divisibility relation on nonnegative integers,
      for which  partial but not a total order  would have been correct. Yes, this was a bit of a trick question.
