Suppose that there is a game that entails unstacking a tower of \(n \) blocks. A move entails splitting a stack of \(k \) blocks into two stacks of \(a \) and \(k-a \) blocks. Also define the score for a move to be the product of the number of blocks in the created substacks (e.g. \(k(a-k)\)). The overall score for the game is the sum of the scores accumulated over all the moves.  
  Which of the following is true about the overall score?  
  

 
 Exercise 1 
   &nbsp;   The score-maximizing strategy is to always split a stack in half.  &nbsp;  

   &nbsp;   The overall score is the same however you unstack the blocks.  &nbsp;  

   &nbsp;   The overall score for a \(n \)-stack game is \(\frac{n(n-1)}{2} \).  &nbsp;  

   &nbsp;   The base case is a 2-block stack, which takes one split to unstack.  &nbsp;
