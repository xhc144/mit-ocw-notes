Toss 51 fair coins.

 
 
 
 What is the expected number of heads? Please answer in the form of a decimal.

 
 Exercise 1 
   &nbsp;  Numerical Response     &nbsp;  

 

 


 Let \(X\) be the number of heads. Then \(X\) has a binomial distribution, where \(E[X]=np=51\cdot\frac{1}{2}=25.5\)





 
 
 What is the probability of getting exactly 25 heads? Please answer in the form of a decimal with 4 significant digits.

 
 Exercise 2 
   &nbsp;  Numerical Response     &nbsp;  

 

 


 Let \(X\) be as above. Then \(\Pr[X=25]=PDF_X(25)=\binom{51}{25}\cdot\frac{1}{2^{51}}\)
