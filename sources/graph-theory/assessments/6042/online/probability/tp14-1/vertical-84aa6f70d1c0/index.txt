 \(\begin{align}\Pr[X_i= \pm i\cdot 2^i] = f(i)\\\Pr[X_i=0]= 1-2f(i)\end{align}\) 

 where \(0\leq f(i) \leq \frac{1}{2}\).

 Let \(S_n:=X_1+X_2+\ldots + X_n\).

 
 What is \(E[S_n]\)?
