                      18.950 Homework 7



1. (10 points) Complete the proof of the existence of local normal coordinates
(Corollary 21.3). You can use the beginning sketched in the lecture (in which
case you need to summarize it for the beneﬁt of the grader), or roll your own.

2. (4 points) Let f : U → Rn+1 be a hypersurface patch. Assume that 0 ∈ U,
and that the second fundamental form satisﬁes gij (x) = gij (−x). Prove that
then, the metric is in normal coordinates around that point.

3. (6 points) Suppose that f : U → R3 is a surface patch, which is in normal
coordinates at x0. Write down a simple formula for the Gauss curvature at
that point.
