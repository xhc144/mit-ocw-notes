18.950 Homework 4



Problem 1. Prove that Λ2(L) vanishes if and only if L has rank ≤ 1.

Problem 2. Let L : R3 → R3 be an invertible linear map. Prove that with
a suitable choice of basis of Λ2(R3), the map Λ2(L) : Λ2(R3) → Λ2(R3) turns
into (L−1)trdet(L).

Problem 3. (2 points) Determine the ﬁrst and second fundamental form,
as well as the principal curvatures, of the cylinder f  : R2 → R3 , f(s, t) =
(s, cos(t), sin(t)).

Problem 4. (4 points) Suppose that f parametrizes a piece of the standard
sphere, so that the unit normal vector at each point is ν(x) = −f(x). From
this fact, deduce that all principal curvatures are equal to +1.

Problem 5. (6 points) Deﬁne the third fundamental form of a hypersurface
at a point x to be
                          IIIx(X, Y ) = hKX, Y i,

where the coeﬃcients of K are kij (x) = h∂xi ν, ∂xj νi. Prove that IIIx(X, Y ) =
Ix(L2X, Y ).
