                      18.950 Homework 3


Problem 1. (3 points) Write down explicitly a curve c : [0, ∞) → R2 such
that the curvature κ(t) goes to inﬁnity as t →∞.

Problem 2.  (7 points) Let c  : R → R2 be a closed curve of period 5.
Suppose that it also satisﬁes

                                 cos α − sin α
                        c(t + 1) =                      c(t),
                                     sin α   cos α

where α = 2π/5. What can one say about the rotation number of c?

Problem 3. (10 points) A polygonal curve is a map c : I → R2 with the
property that there are t1 < · · · < tm in I such that

          c(t) = c0t + v0 for t ≤ t1, c(t) = c1 + tv1 for t1 ≤ t ≤ t2, . . .

Here ci ∈ R2, and vi are nonzero vectors in R2 . Moreover, (vi, vi+1) should
never point in opposite directions.

Deﬁne an appropriate notion of curvature for a polygonal curve, and of total
curvature for a closed polygonal curve (of course, deﬁning closed polygonal
curves ﬁrst!). Does the Hopf Umlaufsatz still hold?  Is there a version of
Proposition 6.3 from the class?  (for the last two questions, answers with
sketch proofs are enough).
