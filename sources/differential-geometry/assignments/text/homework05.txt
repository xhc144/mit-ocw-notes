1.  (10 points) Let f be a hypersurface patch. Suppose that f  lies in the
half-plane {yn+1 ≥ 0} ⊂ Rn+1 , and that f  is tangent to the hyperplane
{yn+1 = 0} at x = 0. Prove that then, the principal curvatures at x = 0
satisfy λiλj ≥ 0 for all i, j.

2. (3 points) Let f be a hypersurface patch of the form f(x) = (x, φ(x)) for
some φ : U → R. Suppose that at the origin x = 0, both φ and Dφ vanish.
Compute the Christoﬀel symbols and their (ﬁrst order) derivatives at that
point.
