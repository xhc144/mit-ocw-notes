IV. (10 points) Elaine’s trusty old car has three states: broken (in Elaine’s 
possession), working (in Elaine’s possession), and in the shop. Denote 
these states B, W, and S. 
1. Each morning the car starts out B, it has a .5 chance of staying B 
and a .5 chance of switching to S by the next morning. 
2. Each morning the car starts out S, it has a .75 chance of staying S 
and a .25 chance of switching to W by the next morning. 
3. Each morning the car starts out W, it has a .75 chance of staying W, 
and a .25 chance of switching to B by the next morning. 
Over the long term, on what percentage of days does the car start out in 
state W?
