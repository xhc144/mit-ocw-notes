9. (10 points) A certain athletically challenged monkey has diﬃculty climbing a ladder. The 
ladder has seven rungs. At each given second, the monkey can be on any of the seven rungs 
of the ladder — or on the “zeroth” rung, meaning on the ground). If 0 ≤ m ≤ 6 and the 
monkey is on the mth rung of the ladder at a given second, then at the next second the 
monkey will be at rung m + 1 with probability 1/2 and back at rung 0 with probability 1/2. 
If the monkey is at level 7, then with probability 1 the monkey will go back to 0 at the next 
step. 
(a) Give the 8 by 8 matrix describing the monkey’s transition probabilities. (You don’t 
have to write all 64 entries. Just write the entries that are non-zero.) 
(b) If the monkey is at state zero at a given time, which is the probability that the monkey 
is on the 7th rung of the ladder seven seconds later. (That is, what is the probability 
the monkey goes straight from the bottom to the top without falling once?) 
(c) Compute (π0, π1, . . . , π7) where πi is the fraction of the time the monkey spends in 
state i, over the long term. (Hint: ﬁrst see if you can express each πj as an integer
