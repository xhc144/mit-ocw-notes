(b) Jill is applying to 25 diﬀerent out-of-state medical schools and has a 1/5 chance 
(independently) of being invited for an interview at each school. Let X be the number of 
medical schools at which she is invited to interview. Compute E[X] and Var[X]. 
ANSWER: The number of interviews is binomial with parameter n = 25 and p = 1/5. So 
E[X] = np = 5 and Var[X] = np(1 − p) = 4.
