Memoryless property for geometric random variables



    I Similar property holds for geometric random variables.

    I If we plan to toss a coin until the ﬁrst heads comes up, then
      we have a .5 chance to get a heads in one step, a .25 chance
      in two steps, etc.
    I Given that the ﬁrst 5 tosses are all tails, there is conditionally
      a .5 chance we get our ﬁrst heads on the 6th toss, a .25
      chance on the 7th toss, etc.
    I Despite our having had ﬁve tails in a row, our expectation of
      the amount of time remaining until we see a heads is the
      same as it originally was.
