We learned the interference of two EM waves to N EM waves.




We call the interference of infinite number of EM waves “diffraction”.




We have ∞ point like spherical EM wave sources. This situation: we will see the “interference”
between all the spherical wave sources.

Feynman: No one has ever been able to define the difference between interference and diffrac-
tion satisfactorily. It is just a question of usage.

Usually we use “interference” when we are talking about a few sources and “diffraction” when
we are talking about many sources.
