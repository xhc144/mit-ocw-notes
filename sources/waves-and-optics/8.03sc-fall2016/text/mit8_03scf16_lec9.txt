*Look at the structure of this normal mode solution. Let’s stop and think about what we have
learned:
(1) Each point mass on the string is oscillating harmonically (only up and down; not in the horizontal
direction!) at the same frequency and phase!
(2) Their relative amplitude: sine function! (The same as the discrete system)
