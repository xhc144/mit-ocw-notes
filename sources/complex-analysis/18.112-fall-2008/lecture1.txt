◮ Finally we show that the spherical representation z 7→ Z is conformal. This 
means that if l and m are two lines in the plane intersecting in z at an angle α, 
then the corresponding circles C and D through N and Z intersect Z at the same 
angle α. Consider the tangent plane π to S at the point N. the plane through Z 
and l intersects π in a line l ′ . Similarly the plane through Z and m intersect π in 
m ′ . Clearly l ′ and m ′ intersect at N at the same angle α. Since they are tangents 
to C and D at N, C and D must intersect at the angle α both at N and at Z.
