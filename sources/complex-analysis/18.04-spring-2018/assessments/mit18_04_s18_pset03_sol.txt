Problem 8. (10 points) 
Are the following simply connected? 
(i) The punctured plane. 
(ii) The cut plane: C −{nonnegative real axis}. 
(iii) The part of the plane inside a circle. 
(iv) The part of the plane outside a circle. 
(i) No, not simply connected. There is a hole at the origin. Equivalently, a loop around 0 
cannot be contracted to a point. 
(ii) Yes, simply connected. Since the cut goes to infinity there are no wholes. Equivalently, 
every curve in the cut plane can be contracted to a point. 
(iii) Yes, a disk is simply connected. 
(iv) No, there is a big hole left in the plane by the disk that’s been removed.
