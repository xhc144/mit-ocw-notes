and, Y ∈ IR is the response variable and ε ∈ IR is the error term. Assume that ε and X 
are independent and let F be the cdf of ε. 
Let (X1, Y1), . . . , (Xn, Yn) be a sample of i.i.d. copies of (X, Y ). Assume that for each 
observation, Xi is observed but Yi is not observed. Instead, what is observed is
