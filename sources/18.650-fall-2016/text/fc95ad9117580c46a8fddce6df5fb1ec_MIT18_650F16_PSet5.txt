Problem 2

electromagnetic waves. An engineer wants to check if both instruments are calibrated 
identically, i.e., if they will produce identical measurements for identical waves. To do so, 
the engineer does n1 independent measurements of the intensity of a given wave using the 
ﬁrst instrument, and n2 measurements on the same wave using the second instrument.

Problem 3 
Implicit hypotheses testing 
Based on a sample of i.i.d. Gaussian random variables X1, . . . , Xn with mean µ and 
variance σ2, propose a test with asymptotic level 5% for the hypotheses 
H0 : ”µ > σ” vs. H1 : ”µ ≤ σ”. 
What is the p-value of your test if the sample has size n = 100, the sample average is 
2.41 and the sample variance is 5.20 ? If the sample size is n = 100, the sample average 
is 3.28 and the sample variance is 15.95 ? In the latter case, do you reject H0 at level 5% 
? At level 10% ?
