2. Asymptotic freedom in six-dimensional ﬁeld theory (30 points)
  Consider the ﬁeld theory
                               1       1        g0
                          L = − (∂φ)2 − m20 φ2 − φ3                            (4)
                               2       2        6
  Calculate the β-function associated with the coupling g0 . Use dimensional regu­
  larization and minimal subtraction. You will ﬁnd that this is a theory where the
  coupling constant becomes weaker at higher energies i.e. it is an asymptotically
  free theory.
