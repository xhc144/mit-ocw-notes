3. We want to show that


                                   0 = Dµ Fνλ + Dλ Fµν + Dν Fλµ = ǫαµνλ Dµ Fνλ                                      (39)
  where α is diﬀerent from µ, ν, λ. We will show that the RHS can be written as

  which vanishes by the Jacobi identity. We now act with (40) on an arbitrary vector in the fundamental repre­
  sentation φ(x) and use [Dν , Dλ ] = −igFνλ :

  We use the deﬁning property of the covariant derivative D(F φ) = (DF )φ + F (Dφ), which can be checked to be
  true in this special case by calculation to simplify our formula to


               0 = −igǫαµνλ [Dµ , Fνλ ] φ = −igǫαµνλ (Dµ (Fνλ φ) − Fνλ Dµ φ) = −igǫαµνλ (Dµ Fνλ ) φ .               (42)
