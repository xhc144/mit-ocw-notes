Question 1: Chiral Symmetry (15 points)
Consider the Dirac action with m = 0. (a) Show that the action is invariant under transformations

(c) Find how the mass term mψ̄ψ transforms. Is it invariant?
The mass term transforms as:

                              mψ̄ψ → mψ̄ 0 ψ 0 = mψ̄eiαγ5 eiαγ5 ψ = e2iαγ5 mψ̄ψ

Since α is arbitrary, this is not left invariant under the chiral rotation.
