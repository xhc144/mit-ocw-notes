# MIT OCW source corpus and redistribution terms

Frozen corpus: MIT 8.323 Relativistic Quantum Field Theory I, Spring 2023 (Hong Liu), and 8.324 Relativistic Quantum Field Theory II, Fall 2010 (Hong Liu). The 73 official PDFs total 435 pages: 24 homework/solution files and 9 recitations from 2023; 26 lecture notes and 14 homework/solution files from 2010. Each PDF and its extracted TXT has a record in `source-manifest.json`, including exact source URL, listing/resource page, retrieval time, PDF SHA256, byte size, and page count. Original PDF bytes are preserved.

Current MIT OCW terms apply [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/): attribution, noncommercial use, and share alike. Retain attribution to Massachusetts Institute of Technology / MIT OpenCourseWare, the specific course and instructor Hong Liu, and Wentao Cui for the 2023 recitations explicitly carrying that signature. The 2010 lecture-note PDFs explicitly name Hong Liu. Homework and solution PDFs do not establish a separate individual author; course attribution does not assert authorship of every solution. Translations and expanded solutions must state that they are Chinese translations/reorganizations with independent additions and carry the same applicable license. Preserve historical notices and third-party credit lines if discovered. Do not use MIT marks to imply endorsement.

MIT's [official third-party FAQ](https://mitocw.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4414756181403-How-is-all-rights-reserved-content-different-from-the-rest-of-OCW-content) distinguishes licensed “Courtesy of ... Used with permission” content, whose special attribution must be retained, from “All rights reserved” content, which does not receive automatic redistribution permission. Extracted-text scans of all 73 PDFs and their official resource/listing descriptions found no explicit reserved-rights notice. This is an evidence-based item review, not permission to redistribute external textbook pages. Official terms and FAQ HTML and the CC 4.0 legalcode HTML are saved under `license/`.

Assignments that only identify a Peskin–Schroeder book problem are retained as assignment references; the copyrighted book's missing question text must not be reconstructed from an official solution and represented as an OCW question. Known reference-only items include 8.324 PSet 1 P&S 15.4(a,b,c); PSet 2 P&S 15.1(c,d), 15.2; PSet 4 P&S 5.5; all of PSet 5 (P&S 6.2, 6.3, 7.3); PSet 7 P&S 12.3. For mixed items, retain and solve the parts actually stated in the MIT assignment.

Both selected syllabi explicitly state that there are no exams. 8.324's syllabus mentions eight homeworks, but the OCW assignment page provides only 1–7. 8.323 Recitation 9 is explicitly unavailable. No nonexistent midterms, finals, PSet 8, or Recitation 9 are supplied. No video binaries are included. The 2008 Alan Guth course is confirmed by the MIT faculty site (metadata and links in `source-manifest.json`; raw faculty HTML snapshots are not redistributed because their license was not established); the current OCW 2008 course URL returns 404, and those historical PDFs are excluded pending their own license/version audit.

This corpus is attributed and redistributed for noncommercial educational use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, subject to any item-specific contrary notice. The repository's software license must not be treated as replacing these source-content terms.
