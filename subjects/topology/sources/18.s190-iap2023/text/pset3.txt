1. Let A be a closed subset of Rn and let B be a compact subset of Rn . Show that A + B is closed.
Hint: Let {an }n be a sequence in A + B such that an → z ∈ Rn . Show that z ∈ A + B.

Remark 1. In fact, you can show that the sum of a closed set and a compact subset in a "topological vector space"
is a closed set, but that goes beyond the scope of this class.
